# nl-cohesion-editor — the chapter pass

Run once per chapter, after every scene has a final. Your main product is the **book
memory**: it is the only thing that keeps the next chapter consistent with this one. Your
second job is the edits the scene loop deferred to you — the should_fix items — and the few
problems only the whole chapter shows. In chapter 1 this pass made 6 edits (1 necessary,
3 nice, 2 cosmetic) and a full memory update; aim for the same proportion: memory first,
light editing.

## Inputs

1. `book/pipeline/work/chNNN/memory.md` — generated by `nl-memory N` for the whole chapter (bible
   sections ≤ N, glossary terms of the chapter, words glossed so far).
2. `book/pipeline/work/chNNN/scenes/sNN.report.md` for every scene — the *deferred to the chapter
   pass* list (should_fix with their required fix), unresolved must_fix, glosses, notes.
3. `book/pipeline/work/chNNN/scenes/sNN.final.nl.md` for every scene (Dutch only; read the English in
   `sNN.src.json` only for a block you edit).
4. `book/pipeline/memory/inbox.md`, the sections for this chapter (translator notes, CONFLICT lines).
5. `book/pipeline/work/chNNN/cohesion.md` — output of `nl-cohesion N`: a list of suspects, not a gate.
   `C-GLOSS-DUP` / `C-GLOSS-LATE` are mechanical and usually real; `C-TENSE` and `C-TERM`
   were 0 of 13 in chapter 1 (present-tense dialogue, thoughts, letters, the epigraph,
   adjective forms) — dismiss those in one line each.
6. When you edit: `book/pipeline/prompts/translator.md` rules, `book/pipeline/profile/allowed.md`,
   `book/pipeline/profile/restricted.md`, `book/pipeline/profile/exceptions.json`.
7. `book/pipeline/prompts/findings-schema.md`.

## Method

1. **Memory.** Edit the sources, not memory.md:
   - `book/pipeline/memory/glossary.json`: every name / term decided in this chapter, with
     `first_chapter`; update entries whose rendering changed.
   - `book/pipeline/memory/bible.md`: this chapter's `### Chapter N` under `## Characters` (new
     characters, who says *u* / *je* to whom — also in the address table), the `**Decisions**`
     of `### Chapter N — …` under `## Per-chapter notes` (final state, not history), new rows
     in the running-terms table with their `first` chapter. Facts a reader of chapter N does
     not know yet go under `## Spoilers` with `(>= ch M)`.
   - `book/pipeline/memory/inbox.md`: replace this chapter's sections with
     `<!-- chNNN: consumed by the chapter pass (see work/chNNN/chapter-cohesion.md) -->`.
   - `book/pipeline/memory/known-so-far/chNNN.json` is rewritten by `nl-finalize`; check it lists every
     gloss of the finals.
2. **Across scenes.** Read the finals in order as a reader would: a term rendered two ways, an
   address form that flips without a reason in the source, a refrain worded differently, a
   word glossed twice or glossed after an unglossed use, a transition that lost its time
   marker, the chapter title.
3. **Deferred should_fix.** Go through the reports' deferred lists. Apply an item when the
   edit is local (one sentence), stays inside the profile, and the problem is one a reader
   would notice (wrong sense, dangling referent, un-Dutch collocation). Skip the rest with a
   one-line reason. Do not undo a deliberate earlier decision recorded in memory.md or in the
   scene's notes (chapter 1: the epigraph's *gewaden* was changed by a should_fix and changed
   back here).
4. **Edits.** Only a scene with a real edit gets a new version: `sNN.v{K+1}.nl.md` = the final
   with the edited blocks changed and every other block byte for byte. Gloss removals and
   other cosmetic changes ride along with a real edit; they never justify a new version on
   their own. For each touched scene run `uv run --project book/pipeline nl-check N/sNN` (no new
   `likely` flag, no new must-level vocabulary row, no structure issue), then
   `uv run --project book/pipeline nl-finalize N/sNN` (promotes the version, refreshes the report and
   known-so-far; after step 1 it no longer writes to the inbox).
5. **Record.** Write `book/pipeline/work/chNNN/chapter-cohesion.md`: memory updates (entries added /
   changed), edits per block with the finding id they apply, deferred items skipped and why,
   suspects dismissed. Problems you leave open go in the schema format with
   `reviewer: "cohesion"`, ids `C1…`.

Reply with a short summary (≤ 15 lines): scenes touched, memory entries added, open issues.
