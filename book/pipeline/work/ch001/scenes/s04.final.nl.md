## @notes
- Mrs Figg → Harry: *je*, *lieverd*; “Poor dear!” → *Och, arme jongen!* (bible). Tie kept: *mevrouw~Figg*.
- “in a jiffy or two” → *in een wip* per bible §8; the playful “or two” is dropped (no Dutch equivalent that stays idiomatic). Glossed as a phrase because *wip* alone is not in the lexicon.
- b087: “Just—” → *Gewoon—*: translates the source's “Just”, not an added intensifier. “strangled voice” kept as *verstikte stem* (B1) and glossed: the plain alternatives (*schor*, *hees*) mean hoarse, not strangled.
- b088: “acceptance letter” → *toelatingsbrief* (not in lexicon; *toelaten* is A2) with gloss; plain *brief* would drop “acceptance”.
- b089: “froze in place” → *bleef doodstil staan* (A2); *verstijven* is not in the lexicon.
- b090: “They say they want my owl” → *Ze willen mijn uil …, zeggen ze* (parenthetical instead of a *dat*-clause; glossary: *ze willen mijn uil*). “by the 31st of July” → *voor 31 juli* (written without accent to avoid a false unknown; *uiterlijk* is in the lexicon only as “appearance/outer”).
- b091: “I can't imagine what someone must have been thinking” → split per R-INDIRECT template: *Ik kan het me niet voorstellen. \emph{Wat} dachten ze toch?*; “someone” rendered as *ze* (Hogwarts) because Dutch *iemand* reads oddly in this question. “the standard letter” → *de gewone brief* (*standaard* is B1). Emphasis count kept (2).
- b092: participle phrase “Hardly even thinking at this point” → own sentence (*Harry dacht nu nauwelijks nog na*). “wrinkled arm” → *een arm vol rimpels* (*rimpel* A2; the inflected *gerimpelde* is not resolved by the lemmatiser and would show as unknown). “stretched out” → *kwam over het hek* (*uitstrekken* is B2); “expectant hand” → *een hand ging open en wachtte*. *afgeven* (B1) used unglossed inside the stretch budget.
- b093: “I'll have someone over” → *heb ik hier iemand* (keeps her agency; *laten komen* would be R-LATEN).
- b096: last line is the bare *Wat.* with a full stop (bible); *jongensstem* is a compound of two known words.
- v2 (cohesion editor): b090 — *voor 31 juli* → *vóór 31 juli* (L1 of v1; the checker now strips acute accents, so the temporal spelling costs nothing); b091 — the subjectless exclamation *Jou alleen maar de gewone brief sturen!* → the finite sentence *Ze stuurden jou alleen maar de gewone brief.* with a full stop (M1 + L2 of v1: Mrs Figg brisk, not agitated; no clause-replacing reduction; emphasis count stays 2).
