## b092

## b093

## b094

## b095

## b096

- b091: "I can't imagine what someone must have been thinking" split into "Ik kan het me niet voorstellen. \emph{Wat} dacht iemand wel?" to avoid an indirect wat-clause; emphasis stays on "wat", and "\emph{have}" moved to "\emph{hebt}".
- b090: "They say they want my owl" → "Ze zeggen: ze willen mijn uil" (colon template); "by the 31st of July" → "vóór 31 juli" (accent as in bible).
- b092: "expectant hand" split off as "Die hand wachtte." to avoid a participle; "wrinkled" → "rimpelige" (plainer than "gerimpelde").
- b089: "verstijfde" ("froze") may be rated above B1; plainer alternative would lose "froze".
- b093: "in a jiffy or two" → "in een wip" (bible voice §8); "or two" dropped as the Dutch idiom has no equivalent.
